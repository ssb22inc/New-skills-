## Findings

### 1. [major] Resume validation misses edits before the saved passage

- **Where:** `pulsern/ops/read-aloud-worker.mjs:256` · confidence high
- **Problem:** resumeAt validates only the passage at cursor.at, not the preceding passages it skips. An earlier passage can change while atItem, at and atKey still match, so the scan resumes past unvoiced revised content. This contradicts the documented guarantee that edited items restart from the top.
- **How it fails:** A run stops inside question 100 at passage 4. Before the next run, a reviewer changes only the stem, leaving passage 4 unchanged. resumeAt returns 4, the revised stem is never checked or synthesized, and the cursor eventually advances past question 100. Its new stem remains unvoiced by prewarming until a later full rescan or an on-demand request.
- **Fix:** Persist and validate a fingerprint of the entire ordered passage group before resuming at an interior position; restart at zero if it differs. Add a regression test that changes only a passage before cursor.at while leaving the saved passage unchanged.
