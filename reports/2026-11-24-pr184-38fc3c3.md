## Assessment

Request changes. Legacy-client reconciliation can delete saved flashcard schedules and recompute calibrated ability incorrectly. The shown tests do not cover these inputs. Reviewed the supplied diffs and images; tests were not executed.

## Findings

### 1. [blocker] Pending legacy snapshots can erase newer flashcard schedules

- **Where:** `pulsern/src/guest.js` · confidence high
- **Problem:** foldPendingProgress calls reconcileProgress(current, snapshot, current), treating the current server state as the older client's baseline. Consequently, mergeScheduleMap interprets every current schedule omitted from the older snapshot as a deliberate deletion. foldPendingProgress then installs that reduced srsMap. An older whole-blob snapshot does not provide enough information to infer those deletions.
- **How it fails:** An older tab has a schedule for c1. A newer client subsequently grades c2 and saves its schedule. The older tab grades c1 and submits a snapshot containing c1 but not c2; the revision guard parks it. On the next load, absorbing that snapshot removes c2's saved schedule, making the already-studied card appear new again.
- **Fix:** Do not use the current server blob as the historical baseline for a revision-less snapshot. Preserve current schedules absent from legacy snapshots, merge explicit entries using their review chronology, and require a known baseline or explicit operation to apply deletions/undos. Add a regression that retains a newer c2 schedule while importing an older client's c1 grade.

### 2. [blocker] Direct conflicts replay legacy answers with invented item ratings

- **Where:** `pulsern/src/guest.js` · confidence high
- **Problem:** In replayUnseen, a newly encountered legacy row without source/key/attempt is applied through updateAbility without calibration. If the merged log also contains a modern attempt, reconcileProgress replaces the saved ability with this replayed result. This discards the actual calibrated ability supplied by the older client. foldPendingProgress has special handling for unrated legacy answers, but the direct conflict path does not.
- **How it fails:** Two tabs read revision 0. The older client answers a difficulty-1 Pharmacology bank item calibrated at 1700 and writes first. The newer client answers an item in another category, loses its revision-0 update, and refetches the older client's row. That legacy row has no stored rating. Reconciliation substitutes the difficulty seed of 1100: starting from 1200, Pharmacology becomes approximately 1214.4 instead of the actual 1237.9. The wrong ability is persisted and subsequently feeds adaptivity and readiness.
- **Fix:** Preserve the older client's actual aggregate ability when its unrated additions can be identified from the shared baseline and counts, or obtain sufficient scoring metadata before replaying them. Do not silently substitute seed ratings for already-scored calibrated answers. Cover the direct reconcileProgress/store conflict path with a non-seed calibration regression, not only the parked-snapshot path.
