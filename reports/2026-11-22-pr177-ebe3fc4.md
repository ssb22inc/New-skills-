## Assessment

Request changes. The new tour can discard an active practice round without confirmation, and its Cards positioning is not synchronized with the asynchronous queue render. The isolated browser tests do not exercise these full-App transitions.

## Findings

### 1. [major] Defer automatic tour navigation while a round is active

- **Where:** `pulsern/src/App.jsx:523` · confidence high
- **Problem:** The tour effect changes tabs directly, bypassing requestLeave and the active-session guard. Automatic tour opening can happen after studying has already begun because entitlement loads independently. Unlike the previous overlay-only tour, this now unmounts Practice or resets a Today round through tourHold, discarding its unchecked answer and position without confirmation.
- **How it fails:** A returning or legacy paid account has saved answers but tourSeen=false. Its progress loads before a slow entitlement request, so accessPending is false and Practice is available. The student starts a question and selects an answer. When entitlement arrives, the automatic tour opens and this effect switches to Today, destroying the open round without showing LeaveDialog.
- **Fix:** Postpone automatic tour opening until no study session is active, or route the transition through the existing leave confirmation. Add a full-App regression test that resolves entitlement after the student has started an answer.

### 2. [major] Position the Cards step after its actual card target mounts

- **Where:** `pulsern/src/App.jsx:527` · confidence medium
- **Problem:** The scroll effect depends only on tourStep and tab, but Flashcards populates sessionQueue in a child effect. On entering Cards, positioning can therefore run against the temporary empty-queue heading. When the queue renders the actual flashcard farther down the section, positioning is not repeated. The browser test masks this ordering problem by explicitly calling scrollTourTarget through tourReport after settling.
- **How it fails:** Enter the Cards tour step with due cards on a short phone viewport. The temporary heading is positioned first; the real flashcard then replaces it lower down and can extend behind the fixed tour sheet. I could not verify the precise affected viewport cutoff without running the full app.
- **Fix:** Trigger positioning when the actual tour target mounts or changes, including after the flashcard queue becomes ready. Test navigation into the Cards step through App without manually invoking the scrolling helper.
