## Assessment

Changes need revision. The new undo flow can corrupt flashcard scheduling and strand undone cards behind the daily cap. The screenshot capture also has a deterministic locator failure, and the dim-theme Again button violates the contrast requirement. Review was limited to the supplied files; tests and build were not executed.

## Findings

### 1. [blocker] An undone historical card remains gradable after its replacement visit is graded

- **Where:** `pulsern/src/App.jsx:2388` · confidence high
- **Problem:** Undo leaves the original history entry ungraded and queues another visit to the same card. Grading that replacement visit does not lock the original entry. Returning to the original entry permits another schedule update and XP award. Because undoLog is keyed only by card ID, this also overwrites the rollback record belonging to the later visit.
- **How it fails:** With cards A and B, grade A Good, go Back and undo A, then use Next to return to B. Grade B and the requeued A Good. Walk Back to the original A entry: its answer is still showing and its grade is null. Clicking Good advances A from a 3-day interval to 7 days and awards another 5 XP despite the replacement visit already being graded. Undoing the later A entry can now restore the wrong grade's schedule and XP.
- **Fix:** When a card is graded, reconcile earlier ungraded entries for that ID so they cannot submit stale recall attempts. Bind rollback records to individual visit IDs rather than only card IDs. Add a Flashcards integration test covering undo, moving on, grading the replacement visit, revisiting the original entry, and undoing again.

### 2. [major] Undoing a new card can make it inaccessible for the rest of the day

- **Where:** `pulsern/src/App.jsx:2402` · confidence high
- **Problem:** Undo deletes the schedule of a previously unseen card but intentionally leaves its daily introduction charge intact. Queue rebuilding treats every card without a schedule as fresh and admits none when newCardAllowance is zero. The persisted fcNew.ids ledger is not consulted when constructing the queue.
- **How it fails:** Grade today's 20 new cards, go Back and undo one, then leave and reopen Cards or change Focus. The undone card has no schedule, but the allowance remains zero, so it disappears from the rebuilt queue until tomorrow. The unfinished card cannot be regraded today even though it was already introduced.
- **Fix:** Keep already-introduced, undone cards eligible independently of the remaining new-card allowance, while still charging genuinely new IDs only once. Test the complete grade-20, undo, unmount/reopen path against persisted state.

### 3. [major] The lab screenshot waits for a tabpanel name that does not exist

- **Where:** `pulsern/ops/capture-product-screenshots.mjs:114` · confidence high
- **Problem:** The Labs tabpanel supplies both aria-labelledby="tk-tab-labs" and aria-label="Normal lab and vital-sign reference ranges". aria-labelledby takes precedence, so its accessible name is "🧪 Labs & lookup", not the name used by this locator.
- **How it fails:** Running captureProductScreenshots reaches the open Electrolytes group and then times out at getByRole("tabpanel", ...). The lab screenshot and capture.json are never produced.
- **Fix:** Locate the panel by its actual accessible name or stable panel ID, or change its accessible naming deliberately and keep the capture locator consistent. Exercise the capture script rather than only asserting that its source contains the new toolkit wording.

### 4. [major] The dim-theme Again button has approximately 2:1 text contrast

- **Where:** `pulsern/src/App.jsx` · confidence high
- **Problem:** The changed .grade.again rule uses background:var(--danger-ink) with color:#fff. In the dim theme, --danger-ink is #F2A296, producing approximately 2:1 contrast for the button label, below the required 4.5:1.
- **How it fails:** A student using Night mode flips a flashcard. The Again grading action is rendered as white text on a pale coral background and fails the project's contrast requirement.
- **Fix:** Use a dark foreground on the dim-theme Again background, as already done for other dim-theme error badges, or choose an existing palette pairing that meets 4.5:1. Add a contrast assertion for grading buttons in both themes.
