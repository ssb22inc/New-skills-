## Assessment

The static-page capture works as implemented, but it expands first-touch tracking to guide and comparison landings without aligning the privacy disclosure with the data flow.

## Findings

### 1. [blocker] New public-page attribution conflicts with the privacy notice

- **Where:** `pulsern/ops/build-public-pages.mjs:15` · confidence medium
- **Problem:** The generated pages now run the attribution capture on entry. For a tagged visit, it stores campaign fields and the referrer locally; when the visitor signs in, flushAttribution inserts those fields alongside their user_id. The legal page still says, "We do not use it for advertising," and does not disclose this campaign-attribution collection.
- **How it fails:** A student lands on a comparison or guide through a paid campaign and later signs in. PulseRN associates that campaign with the student's account to measure ad performance, despite the site's contrary privacy claim.
- **Fix:** Align the privacy notice with the collection, account linkage, and advertising-measurement purpose before enabling capture on these pages; otherwise, do not collect or associate the attribution data.
