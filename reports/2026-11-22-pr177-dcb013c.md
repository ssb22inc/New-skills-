## Assessment

Changes are needed in the navigation handoff. Same-section navigation offers a confirmation whose promised reset never happens, and a failed exam submission can leave a deferred redirect that unexpectedly fires after a later exam. The added helper/copy tests do not exercise either flow.

## Findings

### 1. [blocker] Same-section leave confirmation does not clear the round

- **Where:** `pulsern/src/App.jsx:887` · confidence high
- **Problem:** requestLeave guards navigation even when the destination is the current section, but applyLeave only calls setTab(destination). React preserves the mounted component and its state when that value is unchanged. The dialog therefore promises that the open question and place in the round will clear, but confirmation does neither.
- **How it fails:** Start a Practice question, select an answer, tap the active Practice tab, and confirm "Leave round". The question, selection, and session navigation remain intact. The same problem applies when reselecting Case Study or Cards during a session.
- **Fix:** Treat reselecting the current section as a no-op without a leave confirmation, or explicitly reset/remount that section when the confirmed action is meant to clear its session. Add a rendered navigation regression test.

### 2. [major] Cancel deferred exam navigation when abandoning a failed submission

- **Where:** `pulsern/src/App.jsx:872` · confidence high
- **Problem:** homeAfterExam.current is cleared on successful scoring or by the specific "Back to exams" callback, but not by applyLeave. After a submission error, ExamCenter reports exam=false, so Home and section navigation can unmount it while leaving the deferred destination set. A later ExamCenter instance invokes onScored after an unrelated exam and consumes that stale destination.
- **How it fails:** During exam A, choose Home and confirm ending the exam. Let submission fail, then use the header Home button instead of "Back to exams". Start exam B and finish it normally. After scoring, the stale destination from exam A redirects the student home, unmounting exam B's results and missed-question review.
- **Fix:** Scope deferred navigation to the current exam attempt and clear it on every explicit abandonment or unmount of that attempt. Preserve it only while waiting for that attempt's scoring result. Test the sequence of failed home submission, header navigation, and successful completion of another form.
