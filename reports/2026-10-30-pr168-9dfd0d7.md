## Findings

### 1. [blocker] Formula protection misses subscripted variables and removes their grouping

- **Where:** `pulsern/src/read-aloud-script.js:150` · confidence high
- **Problem:** FORMULA and MATH_SIGN recognize unspaced algebraic subtraction only through ASCII-digit patterns. Unicode-subscripted variables bypass those guards, allowing asides() to replace mathematical parentheses with commas. Pure-letter expressions such as HR (EDV-ESV) have the same problem.
- **How it fails:** speakable("Oxygen use is CO (CaO₂-CvO₂).") returns "Oxygen use is CO, CaO₂-CvO₂." The parentheses expressing multiplication by the oxygen-content difference have been replaced with a prose pause. This is the subscripted version of an existing test case, but that test uses ASCII digits. The audio gate compares against the already-altered script, so it cannot catch this change to the clinical formula.
- **Fix:** Preserve parentheses whenever a group could be algebraic, including Unicode-subscripted variables and pure-letter subtraction. Leave ambiguous groups untouched rather than classifying them as prose. Add exact-output regressions for both CO (CaO₂-CvO₂) and HR (EDV-ESV).
