## Findings

### 1. [blocker] Greek-letter normalization consumes unary minus signs

- **Where:** `pulsern/api/_speak.js:143` · confidence high
- **Problem:** The separator `[\s-]+` treats a mathematical minus as an optional subtype separator. For example, `Potassium delta -1 milliequivalents per liter.` and `Potassium delta 1 milliequivalents per liter.` both canonicalize to the same text containing `delta1`. The word comparison and clinical-unit comparison therefore cannot detect the changed sign.
- **How it fails:** A passage reports a negative potassium delta, but the synthesized audio omits the minus. If transcribed as `Potassium delta 1 milliequivalents per liter.`, the take passes voiceCheck and can be cached and served despite changing the lab trend.
- **Fix:** Preserve mathematical signs. Restrict joining to recognized subtype notation rather than accepting arbitrary whitespace/hyphen sequences after every listed Greek letter. Add a regression test requiring negative versus positive delta values to fail while retaining the intended beta-1/beta1 equivalence.
