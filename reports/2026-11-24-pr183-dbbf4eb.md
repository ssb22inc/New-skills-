## Assessment

One functional defect remains: the floating toolkit can obscure clinical row labels in NGN matrix questions. The supplied browser tests exercise multiple-choice surfaces but do not catch this matrix-specific gap. Review was static; tests and build were not executed.

## Findings

### 1. [major] Protect matrix row labels from the floating toolkit

- **Where:** `pulsern/src/lab-fab.jsx:33` · confidence high
- **Problem:** TARGETS includes matrix radio buttons, but not .ngn-matrix, .mx-row, or their row-label spans. collectBlocks therefore treats the left-hand clinical-label column as empty space. The toolkit can settle over the beginning of a finding even though it remains clear of every answer button.
- **How it fails:** On a phone, open a matrix question and drag the toolkit to the left edge beside a row label. The radio buttons occupy the columns to the right, so findClearSpot accepts the left-edge position and the 52px button obscures part of the clinical finding the student must classify.
- **Fix:** Include the matrix container or complete matrix rows among protected reading surfaces. Add a browser regression using the shipped matrix renderer that drops the toolkit beside a row label and asserts that its settled bounds do not overlap the matrix.
