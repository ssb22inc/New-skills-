## Findings

### 1. [major] Install capture CSS only after the document element exists

- **Where:** `pulsern/ops/capture-product-screenshots.mjs:125` · confidence medium
- **Problem:** addInitScript runs during document creation, before the HTML parser guarantees that document.documentElement exists. The unconditional appendChild can therefore throw, leaving CAPTURE_STYLE uninstalled. The capture does not monitor page errors, so it can continue and report success without having disabled transitions and animations. I could not execute the pinned Chromium to establish how consistently its initialization timing triggers this.
- **How it fails:** On a fresh navigation where the init script precedes creation of <html>, capture proceeds without the freeze stylesheet. Subsequent viewport changes can then capture the FAB during its 280 ms transition; settle() waits at most eight animation frames and does not guarantee that this transition has finished. This undermines the documented deterministic screenshot hashes.
- **Fix:** Inject the freeze stylesheet into the served harness HTML, or safely wait for root creation before app execution. Fail capture on initialization errors and add a browser-level assertion that the freeze stylesheet is installed and effective before taking screenshots.
