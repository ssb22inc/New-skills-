### 1. [blocker] Hyphenation alone does not establish equivalent speech

- **Where:** `pulsern/ops/narrate-lib.mjs:283` · confidence high
- **Problem:** scriptCompounds treats every alphabetic hyphenated pair as equivalent to its closed spelling. A hyphen can distinguish different words and pronunciations: "re-signed" means signed again, whereas "resigned" means quit. The new normalization erases that distinction, allowing a recording with different meaning through the exact-word accuracy gate.
- **How it fails:** audioCheck("The nurse re-signed after the audit.", "The nurse resigned after the audit.") returns pass: true with similarity 1. Both sides become "nurse resigned after audit", although the narration changes the nurse's action.
- **Fix:** Restrict folding to explicitly vetted spelling equivalents, including the required meal-time/mealtime and iso-osmolar/isoosmolar variants, rather than deriving equivalence from every hyphen. Add a passing faithful baseline and a failing re-signed/resigned regression test. When tightening this rule, bump QA_VERSION so recordings accepted by the permissive rule are rechecked.
