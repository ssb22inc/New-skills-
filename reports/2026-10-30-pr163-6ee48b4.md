## Assessment

Two read-aloud changes can alter clinical meaning and then certify the altered narration as correct. These should be fixed before release.

## Findings

### 1. [blocker] The rate expansion treats digit-bearing clinical labels as units

- **Where:** `pulsern/src/read-aloud-script.js:54` · confidence high
- **Problem:** The numeric-slash regex accepts any letters following a digit, including a suffix inside a clinical label. For example, speakable("Check HbA1c/eAG.") returns "Check HbA1c per eAG." This invents a ratio between two lab values instead of preserving the slash-separated label. The audio gate compares against this already-altered script, so it cannot detect the corruption.
- **How it fails:** An approved question or flashcard asks the student to check HbA1c/eAG. Both server narration and device fallback receive a script saying "HbA1c per eAG," and the server can publish that narration as checked audio.
- **Fix:** Restrict automatic "per" expansion to recognized unit expressions with appropriate token boundaries. Preserve other label separators as "slash." Add regression tests for digit-bearing clinical labels such as HbA1c/eAG alongside legitimate rates such as 12 breaths/min.

### 2. [blocker] Global "out of" normalization accepts misread paired measurements

- **Where:** `pulsern/api/_speak.js:113` · confidence high
- **Problem:** Every numeric slash is treated as equivalent to "out of," although clinical slashes can separate two measurements rather than express a part-of-total score. For example, voiceCheck("Apgar scores at 1 and 5 minutes: 7/9.", "Apgar scores at 1 and 5 minutes: 7 out of 9.") compares identical canonical strings and passes. The transcript changes two successive Apgar scores into one score with a supposed maximum of nine.
- **How it fails:** The TTS provider reads a paired Apgar notation as "7 out of 9." The new normalization hides that semantic error, allowing makeChecked to save and serve the incorrect narration instead of rejecting it.
- **Fix:** Remove unconditional numeric "out of" equivalence. Expand numeric slashes according to their clinical context before synthesis, or decline ambiguous passages; allow "out of" only where a part-of-total meaning is established. Add a rejection test for paired Apgar scores as well as the existing acceptance test for pain scores.
