### 2. [major] Build changes can alter marketing pages without triggering review

- **Where:** `pulsern/ops/seo-page-change.mjs:10` · confidence high
- **Problem:** The detector follows imports from three fixed roots, but ignores the build wiring that selects and invokes page generators. For example, package.json and vite.config.js are not recognized unless incidentally imported by those roots. The workflow builds before this check, yet the check examines committed filenames rather than whether that build changed the published pages.
- **How it fails:** A PR changes package.json's build command to invoke a new generator that writes dist/index.html, and adds that generator without editing the three roots or committed public HTML. - **Fix:** Include build entry points, configuration, and invoked generators in the trigger set, or compare base and candidate public build outputs. Add an integration fixture where a build-script-only change alters a marketing page and assert that review is required.
