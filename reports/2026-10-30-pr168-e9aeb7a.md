## Assessment

One blocking defect: the new parenthesis normalization can erase clinically significant mathematical grouping before narration and its accuracy check. The supplied tests do not cover this case.

## Findings

### 1. [blocker] Do not convert mathematical grouping into pauses

- **Where:** `pulsern/src/read-aloud-script.js:135` · confidence high
- **Problem:** asides() treats every embedded parenthesized expression as an aside. Because speakable() also processes calculation stems and rationales, this removes mathematical grouping. The audio gate compares the transcript against the already-transformed script, so it cannot detect this loss of meaning.
- **How it fails:** speakable("Calculate the anion gap: 140 - (104 + 24).") produces "Calculate the anion gap: 140 -, 104 + 24." The grouped subtraction yielding 12 can consequently be narrated as the ungrouped expression yielding 60. Both checked audio and device narration receive the altered script.
- **Fix:** Preserve mathematical grouping and explicitly speak it, or refuse narration for expressions whose grouping cannot safely be verbalized. Restrict comma conversion to genuine prose asides. Add a regression test using grouped clinical arithmetic such as the anion-gap formula.
