## Findings

### 1. [blocker] Variable subtraction can be mistaken for a prose aside

- **Where:** `pulsern/src/read-aloud-script.js:145` · confidence high
- **Problem:** MATH_SIGN recognizes ASCII subtraction only between two digits or with spaces around the minus sign. MATH_BEFORE also does not recognize a preceding identifier as an implicit multiplication factor. Consequently, speakable("VO2 = CO (CaO2-CvO2).") produces "VO2 equals CO, CaO2-CvO2." The parentheses establishing the product have become a comma-separated list, contradicting the guarantee that arithmetic grouping is preserved.
- **How it fails:** A cardiac oxygen-consumption rationale uses the Fick relationship written as `VO2 = CO (CaO2-CvO2).` The script builder removes its meaningful grouping. The audio gate then checks the recording against that already-altered script, so it cannot detect the corruption before students hear it.
- **Fix:** Conservatively preserve parentheses containing symbolic subtraction, including unspaced ASCII minus, and parentheses adjacent to identifiers that may be multiplication factors. Add an exact-output regression for this formula; uncertain mathematical groups should remain unchanged or be refused for voicing.
