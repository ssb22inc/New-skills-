## Findings

### 1. [blocker] Whitespace-separated arithmetic factors are mistaken for prose asides

- **Where:** `pulsern/src/read-aloud-script.js:160` · confidence high
- **Problem:** The arithmetic guards protect touching parenthetical groups, but not groups separated by whitespace. For the second group in `(2 mg/kg) (70 kg)`, the preceding non-whitespace character is `)`, which MATH_BEFORE does not recognize. Its parentheses are therefore replaced with commas, losing the implicit multiplication.
- **How it fails:** `speakable("Calculate (2 mg/kg) (70 kg).")` produces `Calculate (2 milligrams per kilogram), 70 kg.` instead of preserving the product that calculates a 140 mg dose. The audio gate checks this already-altered script, so it cannot catch the transformation before students hear it. The existing test covers only the version without an intervening space.
- **Fix:** Preserve arithmetic groups when the neighboring non-whitespace character is another group's closing or opening parenthesis, or conservatively preserve the entire arithmetic expression. Add exact-output regression tests for whitespace-separated factors, including `(2 mg/kg) (70 kg)` and `(D/H) (Q)`.
