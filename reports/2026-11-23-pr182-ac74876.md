## Assessment

The new navigation can grade a completed card again through an obsolete skipped visit, corrupting its persisted SRS schedule and awarding duplicate XP. The tests cover returning to an already-graded visit, but miss this duplicate-visit path.

## Findings

### 1. [blocker] Obsolete skipped visits remain gradable after the card is completed

- **Where:** `pulsern/src/card-nav.js:169` · confidence high
- **Problem:** The grading guard checks only the current history entry's grade. When a skipped card returns, finishMove appends a new entry but leaves the original entry ungraded. Grading the new entry clears skippedVisit on the original without making it read-only. Returning to that original entry therefore permits another schedule write and XP award for an already-completed card.
- **How it fails:** Start with cards a, b, and c. Skip a, grade b and c Good, then grade the requeued a Good. Navigate Back to the original occurrence of a and flip it: the grade buttons are available again. Grading Good records another result, advancing a new card's interval from 3 days to 7 days and awarding another 5 XP without a legitimate subsequent review.
- **Fix:** Track which occurrence is currently eligible for grading, or invalidate obsolete ungraded occurrences when a replacement visit opens. Completing a card must prevent older skipped occurrences from writing its schedule, while preserving legitimate later visits created by Again. Add a regression test covering skip, requeue, successful grade, then Back to the original occurrence; verify that neither the schedule nor XP changes.
