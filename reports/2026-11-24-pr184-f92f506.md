## Findings

### 1. [blocker] The save after reconciliation overwrites the recovered remote progress

- **Where:** `pulsern/src/App.jsx:415` · confidence high
- **Problem:** Conflict recovery saves the reconciled blob and advances progressGate's revision, but never installs that blob into the app's state. The next autosave therefore has permission to replace the reconciled server row with the stale local snapshot.
- **How it fails:** Two devices load revision 1. Device A saves answer A at revision 2. Device B saves answer B, detects the conflict, and successfully writes A+B at revision 3. B still holds only answer B locally. Its next answer or settings change writes that local blob against revision 3, deleting answer A and replacing its associated progress calculations.
- **Fix:** Make successful conflict reconciliation update the authoritative local snapshot before another whole-blob save can run, while preserving local changes made during the request. Alternatively, persist idempotent progress operations instead of stale whole snapshots. Add an integration test that performs a conflict recovery followed by another save from the losing device and verifies both devices' answers survive.

### 2. [blocker] Conflict reconciliation silently drops flashcard grades and other non-answer changes

- **Where:** `pulsern/src/guest.js:182` · confidence high
- **Problem:** reconcileProgress only replays missing question-log rows and returns the server's other fields unchanged. Local srsMap, fcNew, examResults, preferences, planner changes, and flashcard XP are not reconciled. The store nevertheless reports the save as successful.
- **How it fails:** Device A saves a question answer after both devices loaded the account. Device B then grades card c17 as Good. B's save conflicts, but there is no missing question-log row to replay. Recovery retains the server's old card schedule and XP, discarding B's grade and new-card ledger change. B receives no save error; reloading brings back the ungraded card.
- **Fix:** Reconcile non-answer progress using changes relative to the loaded baseline, or record idempotent card-grade, undo, exam-result, and settings operations. If a field cannot be merged safely, surface a conflict rather than acknowledging a successful save. Test a flashcard-only change through the actual conflicting save path and reload.

### 3. [blocker] Replayed answers lose the inputs required to reproduce XP and Elo

- **Where:** `pulsern/src/guest.js:168` · confidence high
- **Problem:** applyAnswer stores stem word count and the effective item rating on each answer row, but reconciliation reconstructs the question from row.stem, which applyAnswer never stores, and supplies no calibration or stored rating. Passing row.words as input.words only records it again; it does not affect minThinkMs. Replayed answers consequently use an empty-stem time floor and the difficulty-tier rating instead of the original calibrated rating.
- **How it fails:** A first correct response to a 50-word, difficulty-2 bank question takes 3 seconds. Live grading awards 0 XP because its reading-time floor is 4.5 seconds. If that response is replayed during a revision conflict, the missing stem lowers the floor to 2.5 seconds and awards 20 XP. A calibrated rating of 1600 is also replaced by the difficulty-2 seed of 1300, producing a different ability update and readiness estimate.
- **Fix:** Replay with the original effective rating and reading-time inputs, and preserve those inputs on the resulting row. Add reconciliation tests comparing XP and ability with the equivalent live sequence for a long-stem, too-fast answer and a calibrated bank item.
