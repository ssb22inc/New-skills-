## Findings

### 1. [blocker] Rating regex converts dates following the noun "rates" into scores

- **Where:** `pulsern/src/read-aloud-script.js:30` · confidence high
- **Problem:** PAIN_SCORE treats every occurrence of "rates" as a rating verb and allows arbitrary intervening words, including temporal phrases. Consequently, speakable("Compare heart rates on 7/10 and 7/11.") returns "Compare heart rates on 7 out of 10 and 7/11." This changes the clinical content rather than merely spelling it out. Because voiceCheck checks against this already-altered script, correctly synthesized but incorrect content can pass the gate and reach students.
- **How it fails:** A question, chart narrative, or flashcard compares heart or respiratory rates on two dates. The first date is announced as a score out of ten. The added date tests miss this because none includes "rates" or "rated" before the date.
- **Fix:** Restrict the rating-verb branch to explicit rating constructions rather than arbitrary text following "rates" or "rated"; exclude temporal constructions such as "on" and "since". Also recognize whitespace before a continuing date slash. Add regression tests asserting that date-bearing rates/rated sentences remain unchanged and cannot pass as "out of 10" transcripts.
