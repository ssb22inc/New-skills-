## Findings

### 1. [blocker] PO route expansion also matches subscripted oxygen and phosphate symbols

- **Where:** `pulsern/src/read-aloud-script.js:55` · confidence high
- **Problem:** JavaScript's \b treats subscript digits as non-word characters, so /\bPO\b/g matches the PO in PO₂ and PO₄³⁻. These are chemical/lab symbols, not oral-administration routes. The audio gate cannot catch this transformation because it checks the transcript against the already-modified script.
- **How it fails:** An approved item containing "PO₂ 60 mm Hg" produces the script "by mouth₂ 60 millimeters of mercury". A student can therefore hear oral-route wording instead of the oxygen partial-pressure label. The added PO2/POC test does not cover the subscripted forms.
- **Fix:** Use token boundaries that exclude adjacent Unicode letters and numbers, including subscript/superscript digits, rather than \b alone. Add regression tests preserving PO₂ and PO₄³⁻ while still expanding a standalone route such as "25 mg PO daily".
