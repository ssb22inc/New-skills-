## Assessment

Found two defects in the new normalization rules that can reject correctly spoken passages and cache failed verdicts under check version 3. No clinical-gate bypass was identified. Static review only; tests were not executed, and the imported audioCheck implementation was not supplied.

## Findings

### 1. [major] Rejected compound candidates consume the next valid prefix

- **Where:** `pulsern/api/_speak.js:122` · confidence high
- **Problem:** COMPOUND_PARTS consumes both words even when their concatenation is not allowed. For "Read post-operative orders.", it first matches "Read post", leaves that rejected candidate unchanged, and resumes after "post". Consequently, it never normalizes "post-operative". This regresses an equivalence supported by the previous regex.
- **How it fails:** An uncached option or rationale says "Read post-operative instructions to the client." and the transcription writes the same reading as "Read postoperative instructions to the client." The canonical strings remain different, so the documented exact-word check rejects correct audio. Two such takes produce a cached failure instead of checked read-aloud audio.
- **Fix:** Match only valid compound splits, or scan word boundaries without consuming the second word when a candidate is rejected. Add a regression test comparing "Read post-operative instructions." with "Read postoperative instructions." through voiceCheck.

### 2. [major] Sentence-final receptor subtypes are excluded from normalization

- **Where:** `pulsern/api/_speak.js:146` · confidence high
- **Problem:** The negative lookahead rejects every period after the subtype number, not just a decimal point followed by another digit. Thus "beta-1 selective" is normalized, but "beta-1." and "beta 1." are not normalized to "beta1.". Ordinary sentence punctuation disables the newly supported equivalence.
- **How it fails:** A receptor-identification question has an option "Beta-1". questionSegments appends a period, producing "Beta-1.". If the transcriber writes "Beta1.", the canonical strings differ and correct audio can fail both takes and receive a cached failed verdict. The added tests cover receptor names only before another word, so they miss this boundary.
- **Fix:** Exclude following digits and actual decimal continuations, rather than all periods—for example, use a lookahead equivalent to (?!\d|\.\d). Test sentence-final and parenthesized subtype labels, while retaining rejection of changed subtype numbers and decimal values.
