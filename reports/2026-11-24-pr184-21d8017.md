## Assessment

Do not merge. The legacy-write bridge can discard flashcard updates and corrupt answer totals and ability, while Reset leaves snapshots that can restore erased progress. The new pending-merge tests use revision-aware answer rows rather than the actual pre-change format, so they miss these failures. Review is based on the supplied diff; tests were not executed.

## Findings

### 1. [blocker] Actual legacy answers are not reconciled into scored progress

- **Where:** `pulsern/src/guest.js` · confidence high
- **Problem:** reconcileProgress defines unseen using attempt IDs only, but the pre-change app wrote answers without attempt IDs. replayUnseen partially handles those rows, yet the !unseen branch subsequently ignores its ability result. XP is also reconciled incorrectly against the current row as baseline. When the legacy snapshot lacks rewards, mergeRewards can return undefined and remove rewards entirely; absorbPending then passes that unmigrated result to applyBlob, where the next render dereferences rewards.badgesSeen.
- **How it fails:** Two devices start with empty history. The new app saves one correct Pharmacology answer for 10 XP. An already-open old app saves a different correct answer for 10 XP, which migration 022 parks. On the next load, folding the snapshot retains both log rows but can leave XP at 10 and Pharmacology.n at 1 instead of 20 and 2, remove rewards, and crash the app. The pending-merge fixture avoids this path by giving its purported old-client answers attempt IDs and canonical sources.
- **Fix:** Implement reconciliation for genuine pre-change snapshots, with sufficient baseline/version information to preserve both devices' scored work. Do not interpret missing additive fields as deletions. Migrate the absorbed result before applying it to React state. Add an end-to-end regression using the exact old log shape and independent assertions for XP, ability, rewards, and successful rendering.

### 2. [blocker] Card-only legacy snapshots are consumed without saving their grades

- **Where:** `pulsern/src/guest.js` · confidence high
- **Problem:** pendingAddsWork recognizes only new answer-log rows or rewards.cardOps. The pre-change flashcard handler created neither when grading a card. Consequently, foldPendingProgress takes its no-new-work branch and explicitly restores current.srsMap, xp, rewards, streak, and daily over the reconciliation result, discarding the legacy card update.
- **How it fails:** After a revision-aware save, a student grades a due card Good in an older open tab. Its updated srsMap and XP are parked successfully by the trigger. The next new-app load finds no added question answer and no cardOps, keeps the old schedule and XP, then absorbPending deletes the parked snapshot. The grade is permanently lost and the card remains on its previous due date.
- **Fix:** Detect and merge legacy flashcard schedule and counter changes even when no question answer or card-operation ledger exists. Only delete a parked snapshot after all of its supported progress changes have been incorporated. Test a real pre-change card-only payload and assert the resulting due date and XP directly.

### 3. [blocker] Reset leaves pending snapshots that resurrect erased progress

- **Where:** `pulsern/src/App.jsx` · confidence high
- **Problem:** store.delete deletes only the progress row. It never deletes or invalidates matching progress_pending_merge rows, even though loadProgress later absorbs those snapshots into the account.
- **How it fails:** An older tab parks answers after the current app has loaded. The student selects Reset all progress, and the app deletes and recreates an empty progress row. On reload, absorbPending reads the surviving snapshots, imports the erased answers and counters, and writes them back into the account.
- **Fix:** Make reset an atomic server operation that clears the live progress and its pending snapshots. Include a reset generation or equivalent invalidation mechanism so pre-reset snapshots arriving late cannot be replayed. Add a regression that parks a snapshot, resets, reloads, and verifies the account remains empty.
