## Findings

### 1. [blocker] Preserve implicitly multiplied groups instead of treating them as asides

- **Where:** `pulsern/src/read-aloud-script.js:150` · confidence high
- **Problem:** The aside predicate recognizes explicit arithmetic signs and adjacent digits, but not adjacent parenthesized factors. Consequently, speakable("Calculate (2 mg/kg)(70 kg).") becomes "Calculate, 2 milligrams per kilogram, 70 kg." Both grouping marks—and the implicit multiplication they express—are lost. The audio gate compares against this already-altered script, so it cannot detect the loss.
- **How it fails:** An approved dosage-calculation stem or rationale uses juxtaposed factors for dimensional analysis. A student listening to it hears two separate quantities rather than the multiplication needed to obtain the dose, even when the generated audio passes voiceCheck.
- **Fix:** Preserve parentheses when adjacent groups or other factors indicate implicit multiplication, and conservatively retain ambiguous formula notation. Add exact-output regression tests for juxtaposed dose factors such as "Calculate (2 mg/kg)(70 kg)." and algebraic forms such as "Use (D/H)Q."
