## Findings

### 1. [blocker] An older autosave can erase trial answers after their device copy is cleared

- **Where:** `pulsern/src/App.jsx:601` · confidence high
- **Problem:** Autosaves run concurrently without ordering or version protection. Capturing pendingAtStart prevents an older save from clearing the guest key, but does not prevent that save from overwriting a newer merged blob.
- **How it fails:** An existing account loads and starts saving its unmerged progress. The student taps Add while that request is pending. The merged save completes first and clears localStorage; the older save then completes and replaces the server blob with the unmerged history. Neither copy retains the trial answers.
- **Fix:** Serialize per-account writes or enforce monotonically versioned updates. Clear the guest copy only after the merged version is durably saved and cannot be overwritten by an earlier request. Add a browser regression test that deliberately completes the pre-Add write after the merged write.

### 2. [blocker] The trial handoff reports 'Progress saved' before saving succeeds

- **Where:** `pulsern/src/App.jsx:561` · confidence high
- **Problem:** Both handoff paths set guestNote immediately after the in-memory merge. That renders 'Progress saved' and 'Your trial answers are in your account' before store.set completes. A rejected write only logs to the console and leaves the success message visible.
- **How it fails:** The progress read succeeds, but connectivity fails during the handoff upsert. The student sees confirmation that the answers are saved, then signs out. Sign-out correctly clears the guest key, but the answers were never stored in the account and are now lost.
- **Fix:** Render a pending handoff state until the save carrying those answers succeeds. On failure, show an actionable retry/error state rather than the saved confirmation, and warn before discarding unsaved work on sign-out. Test rejected upserts, not only successful ones.

### 3. [blocker] Fresh accounts retain a null rank baseline and receive legacy promotions on reload

- **Where:** `pulsern/src/App.jsx:494` · confidence high
- **Problem:** For a new account without guest answers, loadProgress leaves s null and never applies migrateBlob defaults. rewards.rankHigh therefore remains null. The promotion effect passes the same snapshot as before and after, so detectMoments never records a rank while rankHigh is null. On reload, withLegacyRank interprets the saved null as a legacy account and promotes solely from XP.
- **How it fails:** A new student answers five difficulty-2 questions correctly and earns 100 XP. They correctly remain Student Nurse during that visit because Graduate Nurse requires 20 answers. After reloading, migration assigns legacyRankIndex(100), making them New Grad RN despite its 50-answer and accuracy requirements. Before reload, qualifying ranks also lack the promised permanent high-water mark.
- **Fix:** Initialize every new account through the migrated empty-blob path with rankHigh 0. Apply legacy grandfathering only to genuinely pre-ladder saves, and establish a rank baseline before detecting subsequent promotions. Test a fresh signed-in account through answering, promotion, accuracy decline, and reload.

### 4. [blocker] The new guest study flow omits the required legal and educational footer

- **Where:** `pulsern/src/guest.jsx:90` · confidence high
- **Problem:** GuestTrial renders no visible educational-use disclaimer, NCSBN trademark disclaimer, or Terms/Privacy/disclaimer links. The completed-trial screen is fully shown in this file and has none of them; a disclaimer inside the closed lab toolkit does not satisfy the visible app disclaimer requirement.
- **How it fails:** An anonymous student completes the five-question trial and reaches Save your progress. They are shown clinical exam-prep results and account-creation controls without the required disclaimer or legal links anywhere on that screen.
- **Fix:** Render the shared educational and NCSBN disclaimers and Terms/Privacy/disclaimer links outside the quiz/save conditional so they remain accessible in both guest stages. Add assertions against the actual guest route.

### 5. [major] Imported trial misses cannot be served by Review Misses

- **Where:** `pulsern/src/guest.js:108` · confidence high
- **Problem:** The merge changes log IDs to guest-prefixed strings, but does not retain corresponding question definitions in the account's question pool or provide a review mapping. allQuestions contains numeric bank or fallback IDs, so reviewPools cannot find the imported misses. Deriving a schedule on reload cannot repair the missing question mapping.
- **How it fails:** A student misses a trial question and creates an account. The incorrect answer contributes to accuracy and readiness, but never appears in Review Misses or the Today review queue, even with the built-in fallback active.
- **Fix:** Preserve a collision-safe mapping from imported trial IDs to their built-in questions and make those questions available to missed-question review. Carry or derive their review schedules without mapping them onto unrelated shared-bank IDs. Add an integration test that imports a wrong trial answer and actually re-serves it.

### 6. [major] Resumed trials backdate every answer to the first study day

- **Where:** `pulsern/src/guest.js:80` · confidence high
- **Problem:** guestAnswer keeps guest.day fixed after the first answer, and individual rows have no study date. mergeGuestIntoBlob then credits all answers, daily totals, and the streak to that single original day, even when the trial continued on later local dates.
- **How it fails:** A visitor answers one question Monday and four Tuesday, then signs up Tuesday. All five answers are credited to Monday; Tuesday shows zero trial questions and receives no streak-day credit despite four answers just being completed.
- **Fix:** Record each guest answer's local study date and aggregate the handoff by those dates, advancing the streak chronologically. Add a cross-midnight or multi-day trial regression test.
