## Findings

### 1. [minor] Shift-cover UI can claim a cover was earned when the bank is already full

- **Where:** `pulsern/src/rewards.js` · confidence medium
- **Problem:** maybeGrantCover always adds the current week to coverWeeks when 5 shifts are complete, even when rewards.covers is already MAX_COVERS and granted is false. weekRounds then derives coverEarned from coverWeeks.includes(week), so RoundsCard renders 'Shift cover earned this week. It protects your streak...' while no additional cover was banked.
- **How it fails:** A user with 2 banked covers (MAX_COVERS) completes five shifts in a new week. maybeGrantCover returns granted=false but coverWeeks now includes the new Monday. weekRounds.coverEarned is true, and RoundsCard tells them a cover was earned, though the bank stayed at 2 and nothing newly protects their streak beyond what they already had.
- **Fix:** Only add the week to coverWeeks when a cover is actually banked (rewards.covers < MAX_COVERS), or have weekRounds/UI distinguish 'qualified this week' from 'cover granted' when at cap.

### 2. [minor] Today dashboard screenshot alt text describes a removed 'study goal' card

- **Where:** `pulsern/index.html` · confidence high
- **Problem:** The public homepage still gives the new pulsern-today-dashboard.png (now 1280px tall, containing Today's shift and Clinical career) the alt text 'PulseRN Today dashboard showing the daily round, candidate monitor, study goal, and progress cards'. The screenshot no longer shows the old 'Today's goal' card; the capture now asserts 'Today's shift' and 'Clinical career'. This is an inaccurate accessibility/marketing description of the image.
- **How it fails:** Screen-reader users/search descriptions hear a 'study goal' card that is not in the screenshot. The same stale alt lives in public/product/product-screenshots.json and tests/seo-guardian.test.js.
- **Fix:** Update the alt text and product image manifest to describe the current card (for example '... Today's shift, clinical career, and progress cards').
