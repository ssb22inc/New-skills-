## Assessment

Not ready to release. The new stage-number normalization can approve an incorrect reading that loses an IV route. The digit-only unit rewrites also introduce an asymmetric normalization concern. Review was static; tests were not run.

## Findings

### 1. [blocker] Stage normalization can erase an intravenous route

- **Where:** `pulsern/api/_speak.js:158` · confidence high
- **Problem:** The unconditional `stage IV` replacement treats IV as a stage number even when it introduces intravenous therapy. It runs before both audioCheck and clinicalUnits, so the route disappears before either accuracy check can protect it.
- **How it fails:** For the exam rationale `At this stage IV antibiotics are indicated.`, a transcript saying `At this stage four antibiotics are indicated.` is normalized to the same tokens. The incorrect audio can pass and be published even though it replaces the intravenous route with a count of four antibiotics.
- **Fix:** Require an unambiguous staging context before converting IV to 4; do not infer staging solely from the preceding word `stage`. Add a negative regression test for this IV-antibiotic example and retain positive tests for explicit pressure-injury staging.

### 2. [major] Unit aliases are rewritten differently for digit and number-word transcripts

- **Where:** `pulsern/api/_speak.js:153` · confidence medium
- **Problem:** The new min/lb/gtt rewrites require digits, but number-word conversion occurs later in the word check. Thus `30 min` becomes `30 minute`, while `thirty min` retains `min`. The added tests exercise number words with expanded units, not number words with these abbreviations. I could not inspect the imported speechTokens implementation to verify whether it independently repairs these aliases; the documented number/plural normalization does not.
- **How it fails:** A passage `Observe for 30 min after administration.` with the correct transcript `Observe for thirty min after administration.` now reaches the word check with `minute` on one side and `min` on the other. Without an additional alias in speechTokens, it is falsely rejected and, after two takes, cached as failed under check version 4. The same asymmetry affects `6 lb` versus `six lb`.
- **Fix:** Normalize numeric spans before applying these unit rewrites, or otherwise apply the aliases equally after digit and number-word quantities. Add positive tests using `thirty min`, `six lb`, and `fifteen gtt per mL` against their digit forms.
