## Assessment

Not ready to ship. Two persistence defects can silently corrupt student progress, despite the extensive regression tests.

## Findings

### 1. [blocker] Preserve more than answer logs from older clients

- **Where:** `pulsern/supabase/migrations/022_progress_revision_guard.sql:28` · confidence high
- **Problem:** Once old.revision is positive, a revision-less update replaces the incoming blob with old.blob and copies only additional log entries. This silently discards incoming flashcard schedules, XP, streak changes, exam results, and settings. Even an appended question answer retains the old ability and missSchedule. Loading the resulting blob does not recompute those fields, so the inconsistency persists.
- **How it fails:** A student leaves the pre-change app open on one device and saves through the new app on another. The older tab then grades a flashcard Good, advancing its interval and earning XP. Its write succeeds, but this trigger preserves the previous schedule and XP. Reloading loses the grade entirely.
- **Fix:** Replace the log-only compatibility merge with a strategy that preserves legitimate legacy progress updates. If legacy writes cannot be reconciled safely, require a reload with explicit failure handling rather than acknowledging discarded progress. Add a database-executed regression test that writes a legacy flashcard grade and question answer after a revision-aware save and verifies schedules, XP, and ability—not merely revision changes or SQL text.

### 2. [blocker] Retain earned protection when rebuilding an out-of-order streak

- **Where:** `pulsern/src/guest.js` · confidence high
- **Problem:** streakBeforeInsertedDay rebuilds history using a null streak and emptyRewards(), ignoring earned and consumed shift covers and saved protected days. Its result overrides the server's streak during reconciliation. The count-versus-explained-days check does not establish that a streak can be reconstructed without its protection history.
- **How it fails:** The saved baseline contains Friday November 27 and Monday November 30, with the weekend bridged by a shield and one previously earned cover: count 2, shield false, covers 0. Another device contributes an older Thursday answer and saves the correct count of 3. When the first device saves only a setting change, this function rebuilds Thursday → Friday → Monday without the earned cover. Monday's two-day gap then resets the persisted streak to 1, overwriting the correct server streak.
- **Fix:** Replay streak activity together with protection history, including when covers became available and which gaps were protected, or preserve an authoritative protected streak when the available history cannot reconstruct it. Add a regression test starting from an already cover-protected baseline, inserting an older study day, and reconciling an unchanged local streak against the corrected server streak.
