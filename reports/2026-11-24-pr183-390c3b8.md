### 1. [major] Include inline clinical diagrams in collision detection

- **Where:** `pulsern/src/lab-fab.jsx:33` · confidence medium
- **Problem:** TARGETS protects HTML stems, rationales, exhibits, and charts, but omits the inline visual containers styled as .exh-wrap and .exh-svg in App.jsx. SVG text and paths do not match the generic text selectors either. Consequently, collectBlocks can treat an occupied clinical-diagram region as empty and settle the toolkit button over it.
- **How it fails:** On a narrow phone displaying an ExhibitVisual above the stem, drag the toolkit button to the diagram's height. If nearby HTML controls leave that band clear, the button can dock across the diagram's edge instead of avoiding its content. The browser harness contains no SVG exhibit, so its reading-surface assertions do not catch this. The exact label obscured depends on the diagram and was not verified by rendering.
- **Fix:** Include the complete inline visual containers in collision detection, and add a phone-width browser test that drops the button beside a rendered clinical SVG and checks that their bounding boxes do not overlap.
