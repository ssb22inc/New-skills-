NCLEX testing accommodations: who to contact and when

Registration and results · published 2026-08-31 · last updated 2026-08-31 · editorial review evidence pending

The nursing regulatory body—not PulseRN, Pearson alone, or the test center—coordinates a candidate's NCLEX accommodations. NCSBN directs candidates to submit the request to the NRB with the nurse-licensure or registration application and to do so before registering for the NCLEX with Pearson.

This is process information, not legal advice

Documentation, deadlines, available arrangements and appeal procedures vary by jurisdiction and individual decision. Contact the NRB where you are applying and follow its current instructions. Do not send medical records or disability documentation to PulseRN.

The published sequence

  1. Apply for licensure or registration through the appropriate NRB.
  2. Submit the accommodation request and required documentation to that NRB.
  3. Wait for the NRB's process and decision before Pearson registration when instructed.
  4. Register with Pearson using information consistent with the NRB application.
  5. Review the ATT and approved scheduling instructions.
  6. Use the accommodations contact path on the ATT when scheduling or changing the appointment.

Evidence for this process: NCSBN's dated accommodations instruction, the registration-process page, and the official scheduling page.

Accommodations and comfort aids are not identical

Two different pre-exam pathways
PathApprovalAction
Testing accommodationCoordinated by the NRB using its requirements and decision process.Request it with the licensure application and before Pearson registration, as directed.
Listed comfort aidSome items do not require advance approval and are admitted after test-center inspection.Check the current Pearson Comfort Aid List; do not assume an unlisted item qualifies.

NCSBN gives casts, hearing aids and insulin pumps as examples of items permitted after visual inspection, but the current Pearson list controls. A medical device being important does not by itself establish the test-center procedure; verify the list and contact path in advance.

Scheduling and changing an accommodated appointment

The official scheduling page instructs candidates with approved accommodations to call the number on the ATT and ask for the NCLEX Accommodations Coordinator when changing an appointment. Preserve the approval and appointment confirmation records, and verify that the scheduled arrangements match the authorization before test day.

Common questions

Who approves NCLEX testing accommodations?

The nursing regulatory body coordinates the accommodations process. Submit the request and required materials according to that regulator's instructions.

When should I request NCLEX accommodations?

NCSBN directs candidates to submit the request with the licensure or registration application and before registering for the NCLEX with Pearson.

Do all medical or comfort items require preapproval?

No. Some items on Pearson's current Comfort Aid List can be admitted after visual inspection. Check the live list rather than assuming an item is covered.

How do I change an accommodated NCLEX appointment?

Call the number listed on the ATT and ask for the NCLEX Accommodations Coordinator, following the official scheduling instructions.
