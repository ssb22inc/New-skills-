Content governance

A human-owned review process for nursing education.

PulseRN content is educational material for NCLEX-RN preparation. Clinical responsibility stays with the named human owner and reviewer.

Authorship and accountability

Sheldon Bennett, RN is identified as the creator and content owner. Public guides expose versioned source, content-digest, reviewer, and review-status evidence. No guide is treated as clinically approved while that evidence is pending.

Source hierarchy

Exam-format claims prioritize NCSBN materials. Clinical content should favor current primary or authoritative sources such as government health agencies, official professional guidance, and peer-reviewed evidence. Sources are linked where they materially support a guide.

AI assistance

AI may assist with drafting, critique, or explanation. It is not the accountable author. Generated clinical or educational content must pass automated checks and human RN review before publication.

Claims and safety

  • PulseRN never claims its questions are identical to live NCLEX content.
  • Readiness is never presented as an outcome guarantee.
  • Educational content is never framed as patient-specific medical advice.
  • Material uncertainty or conflicting guidance must be surfaced, not hidden.

Corrections

Substantive corrections should update the affected page, its review date, and its cited support. Questions or correction requests can be sent to sheldon@pulsern.app.

Policy owner: Sheldon Bennett, RN.
