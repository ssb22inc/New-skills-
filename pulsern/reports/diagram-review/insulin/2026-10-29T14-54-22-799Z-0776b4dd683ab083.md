### 1. [minor · pedagogy] Distinguish time-to-peak variability from peak duration

- **Where:** Images 5 and 13; step "nph" caption and narration; worked-example wording · confidence medium
- **Problem:** "Has a long, broad peak from about 4 to 12 hours" can teach that an individual NPH dose stays at peak effect for eight hours. The listed range describes when peak action may occur, not a guaranteed duration of maximal action. Calling the whole computed interval the window when hypoglycemia is "most likely" reinforces that interpretation.
- **Fix:** Say "NPH's peak typically occurs 4–12 hours after the dose; the timing varies." Describe the worked interval as an expected peak-related risk window, not eight hours of sustained peak effect.
