## Assessment

Revise before publication: step crops truncate clinical text, peak-window bands have insufficient contrast, and reduced-motion handling is incomplete.

## Findings

### 1. [major · visual] Step crops cut through text blocks

- **Where:** Images 3–7 and 11–15; step crop boundaries in InsulinDiagram.regions and viewFor() · confidence high
- **Problem:** The rapid, short and NPH steps expose clipped fragments of the subcutaneous-use note below the table. The long step exposes the hypoglycemia-risk heading without its attached caveat. The risk step exposes only the tops of the worked-example text. These are visible rendering defects in both themes.
- **Fix:** Place crop boundaries between complete blocks, or hide annotations that fall outside the selected view. Keep the risk heading and its caveat together. Test every step for partially intersected text, not just horizontal overflow.

### 2. [minor · accessibility] Peak-window bands are too low-contrast

- **Where:** Light-theme images, especially Image 1; Curve() amber peak bands and the peak-risk legend swatch · confidence high
- **Problem:** The amber fill at 0.55 opacity produces approximately 1.5:1 contrast against white. These bands identify the expected peak intervals, but their boundaries are difficult to distinguish, particularly for the narrow rapid-acting interval.
- **Fix:** Retain the amber fill but add high-contrast outlines or interval-boundary ticks using the existing caution-ink token. Test the composited graphics against both theme backgrounds.

### 3. [minor · accessibility] Enabling reduced motion during playback does not stop motion

- **Where:** kit.jsx useLive()/Loop(); explainer.jsx reduced-motion preference handling · confidence high
- **Problem:** Both reduced-motion checks are captured only at mount. If the preference changes while the explainer is open, the insulin curve's SMIL animateMotion loop continues, and timed or narration-driven step advancement remains enabled. The CSS media query cannot stop SMIL animation.
- **Fix:** Subscribe to matchMedia preference changes and share the updated state between the player and SVG. Remove active SMIL loops and cancel automatic advancement whenever reduced motion becomes active.
