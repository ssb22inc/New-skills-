## Assessment

## Findings

### 1. [major · visual] Meal icon appears after rapid insulin has finished acting

- **Where:** Images 3 and 11; step "rapid"; Meal placement in insulin.jsx · confidence high
- **Problem:** The plate is positioned at 6.6 hours on the time axis, beyond the curve's 5-hour endpoint. Its dotted leader does not clearly establish that this is an untimed annotation. A student can read it as the meal occurring hours after the injection.
- **Fix:** Move the meal annotation outside the time plot. Keep the product-specific timing qualification; do not position a meal symbol at an arbitrary elapsed time.

### 2. [minor · pedagogy] Arbitrary curve heights imply a strength ranking

- **Where:** Images 1, 7, 9 and 15; shared chart; STYLE heights and unlabeled vertical axis · confidence medium
- **Problem:** Rapid, regular, NPH and glargine are drawn at successively lower heights on one shared axis. Nothing explains that these heights are arbitrary, so the chart invites the misconception that rapid insulin is inherently stronger and basal insulin weaker. "Illustrative curves" does not explicitly exclude that comparison.
- **Fix:** State that heights do not compare potency or dose-equivalent effects, or separate the profiles into labeled rows without a shared magnitude scale.

### 3. [minor · accessibility] Chart axes have approximately 1.3–1.4:1 contrast

- **Where:** All images; axes group; baseline and vertical axis using C.line · confidence high
- **Problem:** The axis strokes use #D8E2DD on white in light mode and #33403B on #1D2523 in dark mode. Their contrast is approximately 1.32:1 and 1.44:1, respectively. These structural lines become especially faint at phone width and fall below the 3:1 threshold for necessary graphical objects.
- **Fix:** Use a higher-contrast token such as C.muted for the axes, at full opacity. Decorative gridlines may remain subdued.
