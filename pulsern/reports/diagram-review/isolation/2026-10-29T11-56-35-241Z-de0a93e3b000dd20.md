## Assessment

Two accessibility defects remain: low-contrast airborne particles in the light theme and a nonfunctional playback control in reduced-motion mode.

## Findings

### 1. [major · accessibility] Airborne particles fail contrast requirements

- **Where:** Images 1, 6 and 7; isolation.jsx — AirborneScene, AIR_DOTS · confidence high
- **Problem:** The tiny airborne particles use C.labInk at 0.75 opacity over the tinted scene background. The supplied light-theme tokens produce approximately 2.8–3.0:1 contrast, below the project's 4.5:1 requirement and, for several particles, below even the 3:1 non-text threshold. At phone width these roughly 2-pixel dots are particularly difficult to distinguish.
- **Fix:** Render the particles with an opaque existing ink token that reaches 4.5:1 against the entire scene gradient. Check the rendered contrast rather than the foreground token alone.

### 2. [minor · accessibility] Play does nothing with reduced motion and no narration

- **Where:** explainer.jsx — Play button and timed-caption effect; kit.jsx — Frame/useLive · confidence high
- **Problem:** When reduced motion is enabled and the current step has no audio, clicking Play only changes its label to Pause. The caption timer is disabled by reduce, and Frame disables animation, so nothing plays. This affects the default audio=null configuration.
- **Fix:** For reduced-motion steps without audio, hide the playback toggle and explicitly present manual step navigation. Retain playback when narration is available, without enabling automatic advancement.
