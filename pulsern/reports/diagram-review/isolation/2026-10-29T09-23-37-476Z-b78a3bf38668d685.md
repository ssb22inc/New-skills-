## Findings

### 1. [blocker · clinical] Room-placement rules are incorrectly absolute

- **Where:** Images 1, 3–5, 7, 8, 10–12, 14; contact/droplet rows; facts 2–3; contact/droplet captions and narration · confidence high
- **Problem:** Contact precautions are presented as requiring a single room or same-pathogen cohort, and droplet precautions allow sharing only with the same pathogen. CDC patient-placement guidance prefers single rooms and supports same-pathogen cohorting, but also permits carefully selected non-cohort roommates when necessary, with infection-prevention assessment and additional safeguards. The repeated 'only' incorrectly excludes that contingency.
- **Fix:** Describe single rooms as preferred and same-pathogen cohorting as an alternative. State that when neither is available, infection prevention determines an appropriate placement with additional safeguards. Remove the categorical claim that every shared room must be a same-pathogen cohort.

### 2. [minor · consistency] Transport graphic omits 'if tolerated'

- **Problem:** The standalone graphic says the client wears a surgical mask without qualification. Fact 8, DESC, the caption and narration all correctly include 'if tolerated'. Students viewing only the inline diagram do not receive that qualification.
