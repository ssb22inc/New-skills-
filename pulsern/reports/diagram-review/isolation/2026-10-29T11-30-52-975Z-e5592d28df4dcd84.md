## Findings

### 1. [major · accessibility] Step captions replace rather than describe all visible teaching content

- **Where:** src/diagrams/kit.jsx: Frame.described and SVG role="img"; step "contact", images 3 and 10 · confidence high
- **Problem:** In player mode, Frame replaces the diagram description with the caption. Because the SVG has role="img", its descendant text is not independently exposed as readable content. The contact picture teaches VRE, scabies and RSV, but none appears in that step's accessible description. The player therefore gives screen-reader users a shorter disease list than sighted users.
- **Fix:** Provide a step-specific text alternative containing the visible row's full teaching content, or expose an equivalent accessible text table. Keep narration captions separate rather than using them automatically as the complete image description.

### 2. [minor · clinical] N95 is presented as the only acceptable respirator

- **Where:** Facts 5; step "airborne"; images 1, 6, 8, 13; ROWS.airborne.wear · confidence high
- **Problem:** "Require a fit-tested N95" excludes acceptable higher-level respiratory protection, including an appropriate PAPR. Fit testing applies to tight-fitting respirators, not loose-fitting PAPR hoods.
- **Fix:** State "NIOSH-approved N95 or higher-level respirator; fit testing for tight-fitting models." Keep the short visual label as "N95 or higher."

### 3. [minor · clinical] Dedicated equipment is incorrectly made an absolute requirement

- **Where:** Fact 2; step "contact" caption and narration; ROWS.contact.room; DESC · confidence high
- **Problem:** The content says contact precautions require dedicated equipment and describes the stethoscope as staying in the room. CDC guidance permits shared noncritical equipment when it is appropriately cleaned and disinfected before another client uses it; dedication is not the only acceptable arrangement.
- **Fix:** Use "dedicated or disposable equipment when possible; clean and disinfect shared equipment between clients."
