## Findings

### 1. [blocker · clinical] Zoster immune-status qualifiers are not tied to the client

- **Where:** src/diagrams/isolation.jsx — zoster CONDITIONS entries and precautionFor() · confidence high
- **Problem:** The needs/unless expressions search the entire stem. For example, 'The client with covered localized zoster has an intact immune system. The nurse is immunocompromised.' produces airborne + contact precautions, incorrectly borrowing the nurse's immune status. Conversely, 'An immunocompetent nurse cares for an immunocompromised client with localized zoster' produces no outline because the nurse's immunocompetent status globally vetoes the required precautions.
- **Fix:** Bind immune status and dissemination status to the affected client and zoster episode. Prefer structured, validated fields; return null when those relationships cannot be established. Add both examples as regression tests.

### 2. [blocker · clinical] The assertion filter accepts rejected diagnoses and rejects current ones

- **Where:** src/diagrams/isolation.jsx — currentMatch(), NOT_CURRENT_BEFORE and NOT_CURRENT_AFTER · confidence high
- **Problem:** 'The client has pneumococcal meningitis rather than meningococcal disease' is classified as meningococcal disease and outlines droplet precautions. Pneumococcal meningitis ordinarily requires standard precautions. The opposite failure also occurs: 'No fever in a client with measles' suppresses measles because the unrelated 'No' falls inside the preceding 30-character window.
- **Fix:** Determine whether each disease mention is asserted, contrasted, negated or hypothetical within its own clause. Do not apply nearby negation indiscriminately. Withhold client-specific outlines when assertion scope is uncertain, and test these exact cases.

### 3. [blocker · clinical] Head-lice classification ignores the stated 24-hour stopping rule

- **Where:** src/diagrams/isolation.jsx — head-lice CONDITIONS entry and precautionFor(); fact 2 · confidence high
- **Problem:** 'Head lice were effectively treated 48 hours ago' still returns contact precautions. This contradicts fact 2 and the standard stopping rule of contact precautions until 24 hours after effective treatment.
- **Fix:** Include effective-treatment timing in validated condition parameters. Stop outlining contact precautions when the stopping criterion is explicitly met; return null if treatment effectiveness or timing cannot be interpreted safely. Test before, at and after 24 hours.

### 4. [major · consistency] Extrapulmonary involvement incorrectly cancels pulmonary TB recognition

- **Where:** src/diagrams/isolation.jsx — final TB guard in precautionFor() · confidence high
- **Problem:** 'A client has pulmonary TB with extrapulmonary involvement' returns null because any occurrence of 'extrapulmonary' vetoes the TB match. The client still requires airborne precautions for pulmonary involvement. Fact 7 correctly limits the standard-precautions exception to extrapulmonary disease without pulmonary or laryngeal involvement; the code does not.
- **Fix:** Evaluate pulmonary/laryngeal involvement independently of extrapulmonary involvement or a separate latent-TB history. Coexisting extrapulmonary disease must not cancel an explicitly current pulmonary/laryngeal TB match.
