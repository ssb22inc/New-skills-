## Findings

### 1. [blocker · clinical] A placeholder range becomes clinical teaching

- **Where:** abg.jsx: compensationLimit() outsideRules branch; interpretAbg() unevaluable check; mixedResult() · confidence high
- **Problem:** For pH 7.11, PaCO₂ 81, HCO₃⁻ 25, the values agree and fall within the supported input bounds. compensationLimit() sets outsideRules=true and creates an internal interval of 26.1–60 mEq/L, whose upper bound is a placeholder, not a compensation estimate. Because HCO₃⁻ is below that interval, offRange() is true, the unevaluable branch is skipped, and mixedResult() tells the student that expected HCO₃⁻ is about 26.1–60 mEq/L. This publishes a fabricated range despite the stated policy that respiratory-acidosis compensation is not evaluated above PaCO₂ 80.
- **Fix:** Handle outsideRules before any generic expected-range or mixed-disorder output. Never display the placeholder interval as expected compensation. A low bicarbonate may separately raise concern for a metabolic component, but the response must retain the not-evaluable qualification and must not invent a numerical compensation band. Add this exact gas as a regression test.

### 2. [major · rules] Coral still means merely abnormal, contrary to the reserved meaning

- **Where:** Images 1–4, 6, 8–12, 14 and 16; Gauge abnormal markers and acid-end labels; CompTile markers · confidence high
- **Problem:** The valid pH 7.30 and PaCO₂ 50 results receive coral/danger markers, and generic uncompensated and partially compensated examples receive coral dots without any critical-severity assessment. Acid-side labels also use coral regardless of severity. These are neither incorrect data nor necessarily critical findings. The chip-colour correction did not fix Gauge or CompTile, so the visual language still equates abnormality—and preferentially acid-side abnormality—with critical status.
- **Fix:** Use neutral or accent styling for abnormal category markers and acid/base labels, retaining the numeric limits and marker positions to communicate abnormality. Reserve coral for rejected/inconsistent data or an explicitly supported critical state. Apply the change to the shared Gauge defaults and compensation tiles, not only the verdict chip.
