## Assessment

Hold publication. The respiratory time-course logic gives a false mixed-disorder warning for valid acute-on-chronic cases. The inline screen-reader description also omits most of the teaching content.

## Findings

### 1. [blocker · clinical] A chronic-only formula fit can still be an acute-on-chronic respiratory disorder

- **Where:** Step 'worked' and generated inline verdict; abg.jsx courseText() and cautionFor() · confidence high
- **Problem:** The chronicFit-only branch says that if the current PaCO₂ is new, the bicarbonate suggests an added metabolic disorder. Counterexample: a chronic baseline of PaCO₂ 70/HCO₃⁻ 36 followed by an acute rise to PaCO₂ 80/HCO₃⁻ 37 gives pH approximately 7.29. The bicarbonate change is appropriate for pure acute-on-chronic respiratory acidosis. Nevertheless, the code says that a new PaCO₂ of 80 suggests added metabolic alkalosis. Acute-on-chronic cases need not fall between the acute and chronic bands; they can fit the chronic band alone. The same error applies to respiratory alkalosis.
- **Fix:** Remove the inference that a new current PaCO₂ plus a chronic-only fit implies an added metabolic disorder. Explicitly retain acute-on-chronic respiratory disease as an alternative in that branch. When prior gases are available, assess the acute bicarbonate change from the prior compensated baseline. Add the 7.29/80/37 case with its 70/36 baseline as a regression test.

### 2. [major · accessibility] The populated overview's text alternative drops the method panels

- **Where:** Images 1 and 9, inline overview; abg.jsx AbgDiagram() non-concept Frame.desc · confidence high

Screen-reader users therefore do not receive the inline diagram's main instructional content.
- **Fix:** Provide an equivalent accessible HTML transcript, or compose the populated overview description from the shared method description plus the patient's values and verdict. Preserve the existing step-specific descriptions.
