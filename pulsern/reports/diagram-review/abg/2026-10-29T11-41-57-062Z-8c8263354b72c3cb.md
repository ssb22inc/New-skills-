## Findings

### 1. [major · clinical] Candidate ordering suppresses a borderline alternative

- **Where:** src/diagrams/abg.jsx — interpretAbg() no-fit branch using tested[0]; mixedResult(); dynamic worked caption · confidence high
- **Problem:** When no candidate fits, the interpreter reports only the metabolic-first candidate’s compensation mismatch. For pH 7.42, PaCO₂ 50, HCO₃⁻ 31, it reports a mixed disorder without a borderline caution because PaCO₂ exceeds the metabolic-alkalosis estimate by 3.1 mmHg. But chronic respiratory acidosis predicts HCO₃⁻ 28 ± 2: the measured 31 is only 1 mEq/L outside, meeting this code’s own borderline policy. The output hides that alternative and presents stronger evidence against a single disorder than its own rules support.
- **Fix:** Preserve all plausible candidates when none fits. Surface borderline respiratory time-course alternatives and their uncertainty rather than deriving the entire conclusion from tested[0]. Add this gas as a regression test.

### 2. [major · accessibility] Screen-reader descriptions omit the values shown on the gauges

- **Where:** Images 2–4 and 10–12; kit.jsx Frame described selection; ABG steps ph, lungs and kidneys · confidence high
- **Problem:** Frame replaces the data-bearing overview description with each generic step caption. Consequently, the accessible image descriptions for these steps omit pH 7.30, PaCO₂ 50 and HCO₃⁻ 24. The SVG is exposed as one role="img" object, so its internal text cannot be relied on to supply the missing information. Sighted students receive patient-specific markers while screen-reader users receive only general teaching until the worked step.
- **Fix:** Generate parameter-aware step descriptions containing the displayed value, units where applicable, reference range and position relative to that range. Pass those descriptions through StepContext separately from the visible teaching captions.

### 3. [minor · rules] Any non-normal result is coloured as critical

- **Where:** src/diagrams/abg.jsx — bad and result Chip colour; kit.jsx Gauge valueBad colouring · confidence high
- **Problem:** The result chip uses coral whenever disorder is not "normal", including mild or unresolved patterns. For example, pH 7.44, PaCO₂ 40, HCO₃⁻ 26.3 produces a coral metabolic-alkalosis-pattern chip and danger-coloured bicarbonate marker, although the reading explicitly allows a mild disorder and cannot establish compensation. A reference-range excursion or uncertain interpretation is not automatically an incorrect or critical state under the project’s colour rules.
- **Fix:** Choose colour from explicit meaning and severity, not disorder !== "normal" or value outside the reference range. Use neutral styling or amber for uncertainty and reserve coral for demonstrably incorrect or critical states.
