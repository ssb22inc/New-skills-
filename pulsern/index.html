Retrieval practice

You learn by pulling the answer out of memory. You commit to an answer, then read the rationale. Flashcards ask you to recall before the answer is shown. It's the same loop in the Today round, in practice sessions, and on the cards.

Spaced repetition

What you miss comes back on a real calendar date, right before you'd forget it, so it's there on exam day. Cards you know move further out. Cards you miss stay close. The schedule is the study plan.

Adaptive practice

PulseRN tracks your results across the eight NCSBN Client Needs categories and focuses practice on your weaker areas. Question difficulty follows your answers: each answer updates an estimate of your level, and that estimate guides the next item.

A plan for each day

The Today round is one focused session: due flashcards, then adaptive questions, with the next action in view. Set an exam date and the weekly plan is built around it, so you always know what to do next. The lab reference opens over the question, so you can check a value without leaving it.

The study library

The library includes 10,000+ practice questions, 500+ case studies, and 1,100+ flashcards, with dosage calculation practice and an AI tutor when you want another explanation. Case studies follow the NCSBN clinical judgment measurement model. Practice items are original. PulseRN does not use, reproduce, or claim access to live NCLEX questions.

Free sample questions

Try the 20-question free NCLEX-RN practice test hub, or open a five-question set for pharmacology, prioritization, dosage calculation, or NGN bow-tie items. Each free set shows the answer, the rationale, and the sources it was written against.

Readiness is an estimate

Paid plans include a set number of fixed 85-question readiness self-assessments, and each form is shown once per account. Your readiness score comes from your own answers and tells you what to review next. It is an estimate, not a prediction of your exam result.
